## Visualization 1: Licenses Issued Per Year by Status (Interactive)

This visualization displays the number of Illinois professional licenses issued each year, broken down by license status (e.g., *Active*, *Not Renewed*, *Cancelled*, etc.). The x-axis uses an **ordinal** encoding of the issue year, the y-axis uses a **quantitative** encoding of the count of licenses issued in that year, and color uses a **nominal** encoding of the license status category. I chose the `tableau10` color scheme because it provides clearly distinct hues for each categorical status, making it easy to differentiate the lines without any colors appearing to imply an ordering or magnitude relationship between the statuses.

On the data side, I parsed the `Original Issue Date` column into a datetime format using `pd.to_datetime()` and extracted the four-digit year into a new `Issue Year` column. I filtered the data to years between 1970 and 2023 to remove any implausible entries, then grouped by year and status to produce a count for each combination. I kept only the top 5 most frequent statuses to avoid visual clutter.

The interactivity here is a **legend click-to-highlight** selection: clicking on any status in the legend fades all other lines to near-transparency, isolating the selected status. This makes the chart much more readable because the overlapping trend lines for multiple statuses are otherwise hard to untangle; the interaction lets a viewer focus on whichever status they care about most without needing a separate chart for each one.

## Visualization 2: Top 15 Most Common License Types (Bar Chart)

This chart is a static horizontal bar chart showing the 15 most frequently occurring professional license types in the dataset. The x-axis encodes the **quantitative** count of licenses, the y-axis encodes the **nominal** license type sorted in descending order of count, and the fill color also encodes the **quantitative** count using a sequential `blues` color scale. The sequential blue scheme reinforces the ranking already conveyed by bar length — darker blue intuitively signals higher counts — while remaining accessible to viewers with common forms of color vision deficiency since it relies on a single hue's luminance rather than hue contrast.

For the data transformation, I used pandas `value_counts()` on the `License Type` column and took the top 15 results, then reset the index to produce a clean two-column DataFrame suitable for Altair. No date parsing was required for this chart.
